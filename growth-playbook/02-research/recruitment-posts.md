## 1. Reddit · r/Renters / r/movingout(租客 12–15 人)

**Title:** Paid research ($25): how do you handle guests, keys & visitors in your rental?

**Body:**
> Hey everyone — I'm researching how renters handle "getting people in": visitors who can't find your unit, spare keys floating around, landlord rules about changing locks, that kind of thing.
>
> I'd love to hear your stories in a 30–40 min video call. **$25 Amazon gift card** for your time. No sales pitch, nothing to buy — I'm building a tool in this space and want to understand real experiences before writing more code. Happy to share what I learn back with the community.
>
> Interested? Fill this 2-min screener: [SCREENER_LINK]
>
> (Mods: happy to remove if this violates any rules — DM'd in advance.)

**筛选问卷引言(英文,置于 Typeform 开头):**
> Thanks for your interest! 6 quick questions to see if you're a fit. This takes about 2 minutes. If selected, we'll email you within 48h to schedule.

**Screener 题目(与 [jtbd-interview-guide.md](jtbd-interview-guide.md) §2 对应):**
1. In the past 12 months, have you moved, changed locks, or lost a key? (Yes/No)
2. Your housing: renting apartment / renting house / own
3. Do you currently use any smart lock? (Yes / No but considered / Never considered)
4. How do visitors usually get into your building/unit? (multiselect: I buzz them / meet them / spare key / lockbox / they call me)
5. Age range · 6. City & country

## 4. 运营细则

- 每渠道先发 1 帖,48h 看转化再放量;避免多群同发刷屏(会被当 marketing 拉黑)。
- 回复者先进 Notion/Airtable 表:渠道→screener→入选→已排期→已完成→已付卡(状态闭环)。
- 作废比例经验值 30%:招募按 1.5× 超额发。
- 访谈者 = 非创始人最佳;创始人亲自做时,披露身份但强调"I mostly want to listen",防止彩虹屁数据。
